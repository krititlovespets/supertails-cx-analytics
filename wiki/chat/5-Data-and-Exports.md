## What the script needs from an export

Required columns: `ticket_id, created_at, agent_name, agent_assigned_at, resolved_by, resolved_at, last_agent_assignment, agent_frt_response_at, channel_name`.
Timestamps as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`. Rows are filtered to `channel_name == "UCJ SC Chat Assistance"`. The script reads **only** those nine
columns, so a 75 MB / 229-column export loads fine.

## ⚠ Things that have gone wrong

- **`wc -l` lies.** The CSV has line breaks inside quoted fields. A file that reported "929,195 lines" really had 20,177 rows. Count with pandas.
- **A wrong file was sent once**: `CALL_Details_2026-10-23_09_48_02(…).csv` is a **telephony report** (10,689 calls, 39 columns: Call ID, Campaign
  Name, User Name, Customer Talk Time, Disposition Code…). It has no `ticket_id`, so the script fails before writing. Nothing was changed. The first
  rows were the *Pharmacy_IB* campaign — not this channel.
- **Exports can start or end mid-day**, so a "complete" day may be missing hours. Whole-day gap checks do not catch it. Use the
  largest-silence check in [1-Runbook](1-Runbook.md). This produced the **Oct 18 gap** (first ticket 12:53; the previous export ended Oct 17 23:25).
- **A merge step once dropped two columns.** At the Sep 29 backfill, `resolved_by` and `last_agent_assignment` were silently left out of the Raw
  Dump tab until Oct 14, when it was rebuilt from the source files. The script now refuses to run if the tab is missing a required column.
- **Scratch files vanish.** Temp folders get wiped between sessions (this happened on Oct 14). The sheet's Raw Dump tab, not local files, is the source of truth.
- **Stale rows when exports don't overlap.** A ticket created late on the last day of one export and resolved after it keeps a blank `resolved_at`
  until a later export that *includes it* arrives. Real impact so far: 1 unresolved ticket (from Sep 27). Overlap of ~3 days avoids it entirely.

## Facts about the data (verified)

- **Channel.** `channel_name` is a single current-state value per ticket. In the full exports it takes three values: *Unified Customer Journey*
  (the bulk), *UCJ Booking Campaigns*, and *UCJ SC Chat Assistance* (this dashboard; ~17% of rows in the three full exports checked).
- **Bot timestamps.** `bot_frt_response_at` exists on ~99.9% of tickets and sits **~1 second** after `created_at` (median 1 s): the bot replies instantly,
  so it cannot separate tickets. There is **no** "last bot response" column. `cf_transferred`, `assignment_skill_name` and `cf_ticket_created_in` are
  empty in these exports — there is **no field that says "transferred by bot" vs "assigned manually"**.
- **Assignment history.** `2nd_last … 5th_last_agent_assignment` hold earlier assignments (158 of 3,364 tickets in the first dataset had one). `ChannelID: 7` =
  "UCJ SC Chat Assistance" (the human-agent channel); `ChannelID: 36` = "UCJ Super Companion Bot"; others seen: 10 (Unified Customer Journey), 23, 37, 38.
  Only 16 tickets show channel 36 in their history.
- **Message counts.** `ai_agent_message_count` is 0 on ~720 of 3,364 early tickets; `bot_message_count` is almost always 0.
- **Agent roster (all time).** Akshaya, Daksha K, Dhanush S, Dr Sahithya, Farhan, Ganashree D, Mohammad Rizwan, Mohammed Roohulla, Salman Pasha, Shazali Sanober.
- **Volume is trending down.** Tickets on the eight Sundays so far: 215 (Sep 6), 107, 114, 96, 87, 56 (Oct 11), 26 (Oct 18, incomplete), 43 (Oct 25, incomplete —
  the export ended 21:52). So a low count alone does not prove a missing chunk.
- **Gap benchmarks for spotting a hole** (all verified on the sheet's data): largest legitimate **overnight** silence between consecutive tickets
  ≈ **9.3 h**; largest **daytime** silence (both tickets between 09:00 and 20:45 the same day) ≈ **2.9 h** (Sep 14); the first ticket of a normal
  day arrives between **07:45 and 09:15**. Oct 18 breaks all three (13.5 h silence; first ticket 12:53).
